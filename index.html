A Systems Partner for Complex Facilities

Critical building systems can't be treated as isolated scopes. Fire alarm, security, communications, controls, and supporting infrastructure all depend on coordination with the electrical, mechanical, technology, and construction teams around them.

5K Specialty Systems brings those scopes together under one team. We provide early planning, field execution, continuous validation, and coordinated delivery to reduce gaps between trades and protect the project schedule.

As a sister company to Hays Electrical Services under 5K Holdings, 5K Specialty Systems can also work alongside broader electrical construction resources when a project's needs call for it.

Coordinated Around the Entire Project

The value of an integrated systems partner comes down to how the work is planned and executed.

Early Coordination. Our team gets involved early to identify infrastructure requirements, system interfaces, pathways, equipment needs, and potential conflicts before they become field problems.

Self-Performed Execution. 5K Specialty Systems self-performs its core specialty systems work, giving the project team greater control over quality, field coordination, and schedule.

Continuous Field Validation. Conditions change during construction. Our teams stay engaged in the field to verify installations, coordinate interfaces, and address issues as the work progresses.

Integrated Delivery. We coordinate our systems with the electrical, mechanical, technology, and construction teams around us so individual scopes support the larger facility.

Supporting the Facility After Installation

A system still has to perform after construction ends.

Depending on the project and customer requirements, 5K Specialty Systems can provide ongoing system support, troubleshooting, monitoring, maintenance coordination, training, commissioning, and performance optimization.

For building automation customers, support can include remote system monitoring, alarm management, technical troubleshooting, analytics, preventive and predictive maintenance programming, and on-site service coordination when required.
